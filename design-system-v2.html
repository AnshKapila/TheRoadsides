Color

GLOBAL RULE: Whenever "black" or "white" is requested, use Charcoal (#25231F) and Paper (#FAF8F5). Never use true #000000 or #FFFFFF unless explicit.

One primary, one tertiary highlight, warm neutrals. The tertiary color never fills a button or a large surface — highlight text and eyebrows only.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans, one family throughout, warm and humanist. Weight and size carry the hierarchy instead of switching typefaces. Scale alternates 1.5 / 1.33 step ratios rather than a literal golden ratio — chosen because it stays clean on a 4px sub-grid, which strict 1.618 scaling does not.

Page margins (side gutters)

Content — max 1600px

1920px viewport: 160px side margin, 1600px max content width. Tablet (768–1024px): 64px margin. Mobile (<640px): 24px margin. Full-bleed elements (hero imagery, color-block sections) ignore this margin entirely.

Container widths

Institutional pages: 1200px reading container within the outer margin. Blog article body text: 720px max-width specifically, for line-length readability.

Radius, Borders & Shadows

One radius value everywhere. Hairline borders instead of shadows for separation.

Radius: 3px, universal — buttons, cards, inputs, images. No exceptions, no per-component variation.

Borders: 1px, #E3DED2, used for card outlines and section dividers.

Shadows: none, anywhere, ever. If a shadow appears during build, it's a mistake to fix, not a style to keep.

Buttons

Flat fills, 3px radius, 44px minimum height. 32px horizontal padding for Primary and Secondary. Primary and Secondary buttons must include a contextual trailing icon (not a generic arrow) with an 8px gap. Ghost remains text-only.

Primary

Fill: Primary Green → Brick (hover) → darker Primary Green (active). Text: Charcoal text on Primary Green (9.96:1). Paper text fails contrast and must never be used. Focus: 2px Brick outline, 2px offset.

Secondary

Outline: 1.5px Charcoal. Hover: fills Charcoal, text turns Paper.

Ghost

Text + underline only, no border or fill at any state. Hover shifts to Brick.

Form Elements

Flat inputs, visible focus state, inline errors in Brick. Used on Get Involved and newsletter signup.

Enter a valid email address.

All inputs: 44px minimum height, 1px border (#E3DED2), 3px radius. Focus: 2px Primary Green border. Error: Brick border + inline message below the field, never a color-only signal.

Imagery: 16:9 for hero, 4:5 for card/portrait imagery. Consistent color grade across all photography (see hero image direction). 3px corner radius. Real photography for all "proof" content — Our Work, Founder, Recognition. Never a fabricated before/after.

Motion

One deliberate animation, not motion on every element.

The hero image's ambient micro-motion (leaves, dappled light) is the one sustained animation on the site. Everywhere else: instant or 200ms ease transitions on hover only. No scroll-triggered fade-up/slide-in applied uniformly to every section — that pattern alone is one of the most common signs of a templated AI-built site.

What to Avoid

Checked against known tells of a generic, templated, AI-built site.

AVOID
  • ALL-CAPS tracked-out eyebrow labels above every heading
  • Numbered markers (01/02/03) on content that isn't a real sequence
  • Identical cards with the same shadow, icon-heading-line pattern repeated block after block
  • Gradient washes as background decoration (the hero's photographic lighting is the one exception)
  • Button or link text ending in an arrow (→)
  • Fade-up/slide-in scroll animation applied to every section
  • Fake initials-in-circle avatars, or testimonials with no real name/photo
  • Round, unsourced stats ("10K+", "98%") — every number must be real and specific
  • Logo walls with no context per logo
  • Default favicon or leftover site-builder branding
  • Emoji in headings
  • Vague buzzwords ("seamless," "scalable," "next-generation") without specifics underneath
DO
  • Left-align body text and most section content
  • Vary block structure between entries in a list (Our Work, Recognition) rather than repeating one template
  • Say what a link does in plain words ("Read the case study")
  • Include a real registered address and working contact info in the footer
  • Give every Recognition item real, specific context — publication, date, what it was for
  • Keep Brick rare and purposeful — highlight text and eyebrows only
  • Provide one-click access to a real human (WhatsApp/contact) from the homepage
